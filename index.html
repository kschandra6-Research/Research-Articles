Scholar Profile

K. S. Chandra

UGC NET • Contributory Lecturer • Department of Ancient Indian History, Culture & Archaeology (AIHC&A), Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj (RTM) Nagpur University

K.S. Chandra's archaeological research spans over two decades of field investigations, excavations, and material culture analyses across Eastern Maharashtra (Vidarbha) and Central India. His scholarly contributions bridge prehistory to medieval art history, with pioneering works on Nagpur's in-situ Paleolithic horizons, metallurgical typologies of Iron Age Megalithic circles (Naikund, Mahurjhari), Vakataka-era brick architecture (Mansar, Mandhal), canonical iconography (Lajjā Gaurī, Vishnu), and archaeoastronomical records of historical celestial events.
